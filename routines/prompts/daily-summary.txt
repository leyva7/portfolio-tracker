Call them one at a time, with `sleep 3` (Bash) between calls; if one returns a rate-limit error, wait 10 s and retry it once; if it fails again, skip it.
   a) GLOBAL_QUOTE (datatype csv) of SPY, EEM, IUSN.DEX and 4GLD.DEX.
